# XHS_AUDIT_RESPONSE_FORMAT

First non-empty lines, exactly:

```text
AUDIT_PROTOCOL_VERSION: 1
HANDOFF_ID: <task id>
ROUND: <integer>
AUDIT_TYPE: XHS_USER_PANEL_REVIEW | XHS_USER_PANEL_REMEDIATION_VERIFICATION
AUDIT_SUBJECT_ID: <exact audited subject id>
VERDICT: PASS | FAIL | BLOCKED
```

Then include every exact `##` section below. `FAIL` needs one or more findings and a non-empty remediation prompt. `PASS` is invalid when any P0/P1 finding exists.

```markdown
## INFERRED_CONTENT_INTENT
INFERRED_PRIMARY_INTENT: ...
```
